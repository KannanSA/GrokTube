# GrokTube Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

GrokTube does not sell personal data or use data for tracking or advertising.

The app displays London calm-spot information, Apple Maps content, a breathing exercise, and public weather information. Weather requests use fixed London coordinates and do not include your precise device location. Apple Maps and Open-Meteo may process standard network information under their own privacy policies.

Images are downloaded directly from third-party image hosts, including Picsum Photos. These providers may receive your IP address and the requested image URL. Image requests can occur when you open a calm spot, even while live AI features are disabled. Downloaded content may be cached locally on your device.

If you use the map's current-location control and grant permission, Apple Maps may access your location to display it on the map. Location permission can be controlled in iOS Settings. Weather requests continue to use fixed London coordinates.

The current submission build does not send microphone recordings, speech transcripts, or prompts to xAI or another developer-controlled AI service. Live AI features remain disabled until they can be provided through a secure server-side service. If those features are enabled in a future release, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated first.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/KannanSA/GrokTube/issues.
